Restante holds impulse wants until payday. Add a want on the wait clock; the wait snaps to your payday instead of a 24-hour timer.

• Add a want with Title, Price, Necessity, Discretion and Priority
• Home clock shows days left and the payday date
• While waiting: add another want, drop this one, or buy early by dropping another want
• When payday is open: mark I bought it or I dropped it
• Buy early: pick a waiting want whose remaining time covers the gap
• Payday: calendar, pay cycle, Save payday
• Rules: Payday weekday, Pay cycle, Monthly cap
• History: Bought, Dropped, Dropped to buy early, Still waiting
• Settings: Contact Restante, Re-run onboarding, Reset all data
• Wants live on this device as local files

Skip sets Friday every two weeks. There is no checkout and no bank login.
