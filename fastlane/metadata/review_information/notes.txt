I recorded this on a physical iPhone, current iOS, from the Home Screen icon. Restante is a payday lock for impulse buys. You name a want and a price. The clock does not open because 24 hours passed — it opens on the payday you set. If you cannot wait, you buy early only by dropping another waiting want. Audience: people who talk themselves into a cart between paychecks. Value: a lock they cannot negotiate with, and no bank login.

No account, so there is no login / signup / delete to show, and no demo password. Titles stay on this device. Nothing is published, so report and block are not in the app.

Walkthrough:

Onboarding: Hold the impulse / Name the want / Buy early has a cost / Set your payday. I used Start. Skip is the same destination and still sets Friday every two weeks.

Empty home says Add a want. Sheet: Title, Price, Necessity, Discretion, Priority, then Hold this want. The header shows the payday date. The dial shows days left, not a raw hour dump.

I add a second want so Buy early (drop another want) has something to spend. That sheet is Buy early: pick a want to drop. I can also tap Changed my mind — drop this without waiting for payday. Payday: calendar + Save payday. Rules: Payday weekday, Pay cycle, Monthly cap, Save rules. History lists Bought, Dropped, Dropped to buy early, Still waiting. When payday is open I tap I bought it (I dropped it is next to it). Settings: Contact Restante, Re-run onboarding, Reset all data.

Reviewer setup: install, open, Start or Skip, add two wants, try Buy early or drop one, open History / Payday / Rules, then Settings.

Permissions: we do not ask for camera, mic, contacts, location, or tracking. The plist camera line is “This app does not use the camera.”

Wants and rules are JSON in Application Support. Support: https://restante.pro/contact-us. Privacy: https://restante-hold.pro/privacy-policy. No login vendor, ads, analytics, or model API.

Same screens in every region. Locale only changes currency, weekday names, decimals, and payday dates.

Not a payments business, not a licensed advisor, no third-party catalog. No paperwork.
