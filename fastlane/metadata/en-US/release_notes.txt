Version 1.0 is the first public release.

On first launch, four pages cover Hold the impulse, Name the want, Buy early has a cost, and Set your payday. Skip sets Friday every two weeks.

The home clock shows days left until the payday date. Add a want with Title, Price, Necessity, Discretion and Priority. While waiting, add another want, drop this one, or buy early by dropping another want. When payday is open, mark I bought it or I dropped it.

Payday, Rules and History open from the home bar. Settings holds Contact Restante, Re-run onboarding and Reset all data. Wants stay on this device.
